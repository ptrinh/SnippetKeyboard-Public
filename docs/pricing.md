# Pricing

One number to know: **inserts per day** — every tap that puts a snippet into
a text field, including dynamic snippets.

| | Free | Pro | Unlimited |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inserts per day | 20 | 100 | no limit |
| Snippets & categories | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited |
| Filters, drag to reorder, import/export | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Dynamic snippets, extraction, headers & proxy | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Price | free | **$4.99 once** | **$4.99 / month** or **$29.99 / year** |

Prices are in USD; your store shows local pricing.

## Which one?

- **Free** covers everyday use — an address, a signature, a handful of
  replies. Most people never reach 20 inserts in a day.
- **Pro** is for one person answering customers all day. Pay once, keep it.
- **Unlimited** is for heavy use — a support desk, a full-time agent, or
  anyone building replies from many pieces. Cancel any time in the App Store
  or Google Play.

## When you reach the limit

The first day you reach your limit, the keyboard keeps working and shows a
note so nothing interrupts a conversation. From the next day, a tap over the
limit opens the app instead, where you can upgrade. Limits reset at local
midnight.

## Already bought Pro before September 2026?

You keep **unlimited inserts forever**. That was the deal when you bought it,
and it still is. The app recognises the original purchase automatically;
if it does not, use **Restore Purchase** on the Get Pro screen.

## Notes

- Purchases are per store account (Apple ID or Google account), so they
  follow you across your own devices on that platform.
- A lapsed Unlimited subscription falls back to whatever you own: Pro if you
  bought it, otherwise Free. Your snippets are never touched.
- Pro (one time) requires Google Play services on Android; sideloaded APKs
  from GitHub can use Free.
