# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 September 2026_

Snippet Keyboard is made by Phil Trinh. This policy covers the iOS and Android
apps and their keyboards.

## The short version

The app collects nothing. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising
and no third-party SDKs other than the platform's own payment library. Your
snippets stay on your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Your snippets and categories**, stored locally (iOS: an App Group shared
  between the app and its keyboard; Android: the app's private storage).
- **Daily usage counters** used to apply the free-tier limit.
- **Settings**, including any HTTP headers or proxy you enter under
  Setup → Advanced. These never leave your device except as part of a request
  you trigger yourself (below).

None of this is sent to us. We have no servers that receive your snippets.

## When the app uses the network

Only when **you** tap a dynamic snippet you created — a snippet whose content
is an `https://` URL. The app requests that URL and inserts the response. The
request goes to the address you chose, with any headers or proxy you
configured. We do not see it, log it, or proxy it.

Static snippets, filters, import and export work entirely offline. On iOS the
keyboard does not even have network access unless you grant Full Access.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never
see your payment details. The stores tell the app only whether a purchase is
active. Their privacy policies apply to that transaction:
[Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) ·
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Your data rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or
delete. Removing the app removes everything it stored. You can also delete all
snippets from Settings at any time.
